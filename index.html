Chimney Inspections

A careful look at the liner, masonry, crown and flashing. You'll know what shape your chimney is in before you light the first fire of winter.

Creosote and Soot Removal

Creosote builds up every time you burn wood. Albert clears that buildup and soot from the flue so it can't choke your draft or catch fire.

A good cap keeps out rain, critters and debris. A working damper keeps your heat in the house. Both get fixed right, up top and down below.
